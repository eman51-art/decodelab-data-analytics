# 🗄️ DecodeLab Task 3: SQL Data Analysis

## 📋 Overview
This task focuses on extracting actionable business intelligence from an e-commerce dataset using pure SQL. It demonstrates the ability to filter, group, and aggregate raw data to answer specific business questions.

## 🎯 Key Business Insights
- **Top Revenue Source:** Instagram & Google drive the highest sales volume.
- **Product Performance:** Chair, Printer, and Laptop contribute to 60%+ of total revenue.
- **Operational Health:** High cancellation/return rates (~40%) require immediate review.
- **Payment Trends:** Credit Card is the most preferred payment method.

## 🛠️ SQL Skills Demonstrated
- **Data Extraction:** `SELECT` queries for specific columns.
- **Filtering:** `WHERE` clauses for targeted data (e.g., High-value orders).
- **Grouping & Sorting:** `GROUP BY` and `ORDER BY` for categorization and ranking.
- **Aggregations:** `COUNT()`, `SUM()`, and `AVG()` for mathematical insights.
